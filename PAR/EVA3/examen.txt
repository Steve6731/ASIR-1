	--开trunk端口到路由器
	(route)
	--打开主端口
		interface g0/1
		not shutdown
	--打开子端口 v 10
		interface g0/1.10
		encryption dot1Q 10
		ip ad 192.168.10.1 255.255.255.0
	--打开子端口 v 20
		interface g0/1.20
		encryption dot1Q 20
		ip ad 192.168.20.1 255.255.255.0
	允许IP路由
		ip routing

--安全端口
show port-security interface [interface]
Modos de violación de seguridad
1 Habilitar port-security
	(int)#switchport port-security
	(int)#switchport port-security violation [mode]
2 poner el maximo nº de macs
	(int)#switchport port-security maximum 10
	(int)#switchport port-security aging time [num(minutes)]
3 poner como va a aprender las macs
	(int)#switchport port-security mac-address sticky
	(int)#switchport port-security mac-address sticky dirección-mac (conf manual)
4 Que hacer ante violacia de seguridad
	(int)#switchport port-security violation {protect | restrict | shutdown}
5 permite address securo munual
	(int)#switchport port-security mac-addresss direccion-mac

--BPDU Guard
--注：BPDU：Bridge protocol data units
	(c)#spanning-tree bpduguard enable	启用BPDU Guard(如果收到BPDU就禁用该端口)
	(c)#spanning-tree portfast bpduguard default 所有portfast启用BPDU Guard
	(c)#spanning-tree portfast bpdufilter default 收到BPDU可以将portfast转为普通但BPDU Guard 不变

--dhcp Snooping DHCP监控
show ip dhcp snooping binding
1 habilitar DHCP snooping
	(c)#ip dhcp snoping 启用监控
	(c)#ip dhcp snoping vlan [vlan] 在特定vlan上启用监控
	(c)#no ip dhcp snoping informacion option 避免relay相关问题
2 Escoger puertos confiables
	(int)#ip dhcp snooping trust 认定这一端口可信
3 limitar velocidad(tasa peticiones)
	ip dhcp snooping limit rate [nº paquetes/segundo]
4 recupera los limitados
	errdisable recovery cause dhcp-rate-limit
	errdisable recovery cause [causa] 启用想要的模式
	errdisable reconvery interval [segundo]
